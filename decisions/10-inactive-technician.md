# 10. A staff member with no recorded work

**What we found**
Ben Cartwright is on the staff list marked "Active = N" but never appears on a single job or timesheet entry.

**What we did**
We kept him in the system as an inactive technician, so his record isn't erased, but he's excluded from the "assign a technician" picker by default so office staff don't accidentally book a job to someone who's left.

**Ask the client?**
No — the data is consistent here, we're just noting it.
