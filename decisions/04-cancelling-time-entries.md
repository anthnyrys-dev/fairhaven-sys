# 4. Two entries cancel each other out, with no correct number left behind

**What we found**
Priya Raman has two rows for job J-3022 on the same day: +6.5 hours ("Assisting") and −6.5 hours ("Entered wrong way round, needs fixing"). Together they net to zero — but there's no third row saying what her hours actually were that day.

**What we did**
We imported both rows exactly as they are, rather than deleting one or inventing a "correct" number. Both entries are visible in the system, and they net to zero hours for that day. We'd rather show "we don't know" honestly than guess at a figure that isn't in the data.
