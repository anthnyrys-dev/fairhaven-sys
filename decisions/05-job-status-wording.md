# 5. Job status is typed freely, with no fixed list of options

**What we found**
The Status column has six different spellings for what are really four states: "Complete", "completed", "DONE", "In Progress", "Open", "Scheduled".

**What we did**
We standardised these into four fixed options — Open, Scheduled, In Progress, Complete — and the job form now only lets office staff pick from that list, instead of typing anything they like. This is also what makes an "outstanding jobs" view possible at all: you can't reliably filter for unfinished jobs if "DONE" and "Complete" both mean the same thing but look different to the computer.

**Ask the client?**
No — the intent behind every value was clear, this was just messy typing, not a business rule we were unsure about.
