# 9. One client has jobs at two different addresses

**What we found**
Halden Logistics has jobs at "3 Kalimna Way, Kewdale" and separately at "55 Hardey Rd, Forrestfield" — a real business with more than one site, not a data error. Separately, Marlow Group's address is "310 Hay St, Perth" on every job except one, which reads "318 Hay St" — almost certainly a typo.

**What we did**
We keep the site address on the job, not on the client — that way a client with several sites is handled naturally, without forcing every job into one fixed address. The likely Marlow Group typo was left as-is rather than silently changed to a guess.

**Ask the client?**
Worth a quick check on the Marlow Group address (probably a typo), not a blocker. The multi-site handling for Halden Logistics needs no question — it's normal business reality.
