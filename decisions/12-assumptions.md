# 12. Assumptions we made without asking

A few small calls that didn't need a client question — they were either stated directly in the workbook or safe to infer.

**Charge rate vs cost rate**
Charge Rate is what's billed to the client per hour. Cost Rate is what the technician costs the business per hour. This is stated directly in the workbook's own Read Me sheet.

**Quoted amount vs actual labour cost**
A job's quoted amount is a fixed price agreed with the client, not automatically `hours × charge rate`. The system shows both side by side so office staff can see the gap, rather than assuming they should always match exactly.

**What counts as "outstanding"**
Any job that isn't marked Complete (so Open, Scheduled, or In Progress) counts as outstanding.
