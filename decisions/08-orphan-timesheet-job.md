# 8. An hours entry references a job that doesn't exist in the Jobs sheet

**What we found**
Sam O'Brien logged 4 hours against job "J-2988", noted "Carried over from December job book." Every real job in the Jobs sheet is numbered J-3001 or higher — J-2988 is from a period this workbook doesn't cover at all.

**What we did**
We created a placeholder job for J-2988 so Sam's hours aren't silently dropped from his total, and we're upfront about it in the system — this job shows its client as "Unknown — legacy job" rather than pretending we know who it was for.

**Ask the client?**
Yes — is there a December job book we should also bring in, or is this one placeholder entry enough to make the historical hours add up?
