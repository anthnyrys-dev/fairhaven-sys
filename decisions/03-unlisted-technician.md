# 3. A technician logs hours but isn't on the staff list

**What we found**
"J. Fitzsimmons" logs hours on three jobs (J-3006, J-3010, J-3018), with notes like "refrigeration specialist." But this person doesn't appear anywhere on the Technicians sheet — no charge rate, no cost rate, no role.

**What we did**
We added them as a technician so their hours aren't lost, but left their rate and role blank rather than guessing a number. The system shows this plainly ("Not recorded") rather than defaulting to $0, so nobody mistakes a missing rate for a real one.

**Ask the client?**
Yes — is J. Fitzsimmons a subcontractor who should have a rate on file for margin reporting, or a staff member who was simply left off the list you sent us?
